About me

Hi, I'm Ahmad.

I'm an iOS and web developer, and reverse engineering is my craft — it's how a tweak can reach deep into an app without getting in its way.

I take iOS apps apart to learn exactly how they work — then add only what belongs, in the app's own style and in every language it speaks. I build each one around what you feel without noticing: it looks native, stays light and quick, and holds up day after day.

Everything here is tried on real devices before it ships, and every file is checked against the release it came from.
