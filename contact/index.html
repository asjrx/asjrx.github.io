Before you write

For a problem, these help me fix it faster:

  1. Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  2. The app's version and the tweak's version.
  3. How you installed it — jailbreak, a signing app or TrollStore.
  4. What you did and what happened. A screenshot or a screen recording helps.
